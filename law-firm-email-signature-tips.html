Law Firm Email Signatures: What to Include

An email signature is sent hundreds of times a week. For a law firm it is also a small piece of advertising and, in many states, a place where disclaimers belong.

Include

  • Name and title, with bar admissions if relevant.
  • Firm name, one phone number and the office address.
  • The website link.
  • A confidentiality notice, kept short.

Leave out

  • Inspirational quotes and several fonts.
  • Large images that show as attachments.
  • Every social network; pick one or two you actually use.

Keep everyone the same

Build one template and give it to every attorney and staff member. Matching signatures look more professional and keep the firm's name, phone and address consistent, which also helps search engines connect the firm's details.
