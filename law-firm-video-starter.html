Video for Law Firm Websites: A Practical Starter

Most people who contact a lawyer have never met one. A short video lets them hear the attorney's voice and see how they explain things before they pick up the phone. It does not need a studio. It needs a plan.

Start with five questions

Write down the five questions the front desk hears every week. "How much does this cost?", "How long will it take?", "Do I need a lawyer for this?" Each becomes a 60 to 90 second video. Answer the question in the first ten seconds, then explain.

Simple kit

  • A phone on a tripod at eye level.
  • A clip-on microphone. Bad sound loses viewers faster than a bad picture.
  • A window in front of the speaker, not behind.
  • A quiet office with a plain background.

Where each video goes

Keep it compliant

State bar advertising rules apply to video too. Avoid promising outcomes, label dramatisations, and add any disclaimer your state requires. Add captions; many people watch with the sound off.
