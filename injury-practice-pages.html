Building Strong Pages for Specialist Injury Practices

Personal injury firms often lump every case type onto one page. Clients with a medical malpractice or nursing home abuse claim are searching for something specific, and a general page rarely answers their questions.

Medical malpractice pages

These clients are often unsure whether a bad outcome is malpractice at all. Explain the difference in plain words, the role of expert review, and your state's filing deadline. Keep the tone calm; many readers are grieving.

Nursing home abuse pages

Readers are usually adult children worried about a parent. List warning signs, what to document, and who to report to, before you ask them to call. Family members want to know they are being taken seriously.

What both pages need

  • One clear phone number at the top on mobile.
  • Attorney photos and real experience with these cases.
  • An FAQ section with short answers.
  • Results shown only with the disclaimer your state requires.
