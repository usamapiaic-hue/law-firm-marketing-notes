How AI Assistants Decide Which Law Firms to Mention

More people now ask an AI assistant "who handles probate in my town?" before they search. The assistant can only describe your firm from what it can find and trust about it.

What these tools look for

  • Clear facts: name, practice areas, city, phone and attorneys, written the same way everywhere.
  • Direct answers: pages that answer a question in the first two sentences, then explain.
  • Mentions elsewhere: directories, bar listings, local news and reviews that agree with the website.
  • Access: a robots.txt that does not block the crawlers these tools use.

Quick fixes

  1. Add a short "About the firm" paragraph with the facts above to the homepage.
  2. Give every practice page an FAQ with plain, one-paragraph answers.
  3. Mark up the firm with LegalService or Attorney schema.
  4. Fix old directory listings that show a previous address or phone.

What not to do

Do not publish pages stuffed with every city and keyword. AI tools summarise; thin, repetitive pages give them nothing worth quoting.
