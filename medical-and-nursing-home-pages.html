Medical Malpractice and Nursing Home Abuse Pages

Families looking into medical malpractice or nursing home abuse are often unsure whether what happened was negligence or simply bad luck. Good pages help them think it through without making promises.

Medical malpractice

Explain in plain terms what has to be shown, that expert review is usually needed, and that time limits can be short. Many states also have special procedural steps; mention them without legal jargon.

Nursing home abuse and neglect

Most visitors are adult children. List the warning signs (bedsores, falls, weight loss, unexplained injuries), explain how to document them and who to report to, then how the firm can help.

Workers' compensation

Injured workers want to know whether they can be fired for filing and how long benefits last. Answer those first.
