What a Bankruptcy Firm's Website Should Say First

People researching bankruptcy are stressed, often embarrassed, and reading late at night. The first screen of the website should lower that stress, not add to it.

Lead with relief

Say what the firm can help stop: collection calls, wage garnishment, repossession, foreclosure. Then say how the first consultation works and what it costs.

Explain the chapters simply

A short, plain comparison of Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 helps visitors see which might fit them before they call. Avoid legal shorthand.

Answer the quiet questions

  • Will I lose my house or car?
  • Will my employer find out?
  • How long will this stay on my credit?

Follow the debt-relief agency rules

Bankruptcy advertising has federal disclosure requirements on top of state bar rules. Make sure the required statement appears where it should.
