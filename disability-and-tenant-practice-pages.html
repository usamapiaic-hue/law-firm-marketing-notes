Pages for Disability and Landlord-Tenant Practices

Social Security disability and landlord-tenant clients often have little time, money or energy for long research. Clear, short pages win their trust.

Social Security disability

  • Explain the stages: application, reconsideration, hearing, appeal.
  • Say plainly how fees work in these cases; many people assume they cannot afford a lawyer.
  • Use large text and simple layouts. Many readers have health conditions that make small text hard to read.

Landlord-tenant

  • Say which side you represent, landlords, tenants or both, on the first screen.
  • Cover the urgent problems first: eviction notices, deposits, repairs, lockouts.
  • Include local court information; these cases are very local.
