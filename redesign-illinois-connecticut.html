Planning a Law Firm Redesign in Illinois or Connecticut

A redesign is a good moment to check that the site reflects where and how the firm actually practises. Firms in Illinois often serve Chicago and the suburbs very differently from downstate clients; Connecticut firms often take clients from New York and Massachusetts too.

  • Decide which areas each office serves and say so plainly.
  • Keep URLs that already rank, and redirect the rest.
  • Check advertising rules for every state where attorneys are admitted.
