Traffic Ticket and DUI Pages That Get Calls

Traffic and DUI clients search on a phone, often the same day they were stopped. The page has to load fast, show the phone number at once and answer the first worry in a sentence.

  • Traffic tickets: points, insurance costs and whether the client needs to appear in court.
  • DUI: the deadline to challenge a license suspension, what happens at the first hearing, and whether the firm knows the local courts.

Pages for each court or county the firm covers help both visitors and search engines.
