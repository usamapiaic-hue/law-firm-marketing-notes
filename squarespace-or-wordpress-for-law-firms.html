Squarespace or WordPress for a Small Law Firm?

Most new firms end up choosing between Squarespace and WordPress. Both can produce a good law firm website. The right one depends on who will maintain it and how much the firm wants to grow online.

QuestionSquarespaceWordPress
Who updates it?Anyone in the officeEasier with a developer or agency
Hosting and securityIncludedYou choose and maintain the host
Many practice and city pagesPossible, but slow to manageBuilt for it
Schema, redirects, SEO pluginsLimitedFull control
Intake forms and call trackingBasicMany integrations

A simple rule

A solo attorney who wants a clean five-page site and no maintenance is usually happy on Squarespace. A firm that plans to publish practice pages, location pages and regular articles usually outgrows it and is better on WordPress from the start, because moving later costs time and rankings.
