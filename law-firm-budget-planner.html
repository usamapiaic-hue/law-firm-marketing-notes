Planning a Small Law Firm's Marketing Budget

Marketing budgets at small firms are usually set by what was spent last year. A better starting point is the number of new matters the firm wants, and what each one is worth.

Work backwards from cases

  1. How many new clients do you want each month?
  2. Roughly what share of consultations become clients?
  3. So how many consultations, and how many calls or forms, do you need?
  4. What does a lead cost in each channel you use today?

The answer gives a range, not a single number, and that is fine.

Review monthly, decide quarterly

Look at cost per consultation and per signed client, not clicks or rankings. Give a channel at least a quarter before cutting it, except paid ads that produce no calls at all after a fair test.
