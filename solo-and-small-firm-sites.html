Solo and Small Firm Websites: Do Less, Better

Small firms often copy the structure of large firm websites: dozens of practice pages, a news section and long attorney profiles. A smaller site that is kept current usually performs better.

  • Fewer practice pages, each one genuinely useful.
  • A personal About page; clients are hiring a person.
  • A blog only if someone will post regularly.
