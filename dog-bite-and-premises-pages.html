Writing Dog Bite and Premises Liability Pages That Help

Dog bite and premises liability cases share one thing: the injured person is often unsure whether anyone is responsible at all. A good page answers that doubt before it asks for a call.

Dog bite pages

  • Explain how your state handles owner liability in one paragraph.
  • List what to do after a bite: medical care, photos, the owner's details, a report to animal control.
  • Mention that homeowner's or renter's insurance often pays these claims; many people fear suing a neighbour.

Premises liability pages

  • Give common examples: wet floors, broken stairs, poor lighting, unsafe parking lots.
  • Explain why photos of the hazard and an incident report matter.
  • State any deadline for claims against government property, which is often much shorter.
