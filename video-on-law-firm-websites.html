Putting Video on a Law Firm Website

A short video of an attorney answering a common question does what text cannot: it shows who the client will be talking to. It does not need a film crew.

Where video helps most

  • The homepage: a 60-second introduction.
  • Attorney bios: a short hello in the attorney's own words.
  • Practice pages: one common question answered.

Keep it light

Host videos on a video platform and embed them, so the page stays fast. Add captions and a short text summary under each video.
