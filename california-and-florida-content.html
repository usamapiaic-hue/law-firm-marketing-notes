Writing Law Firm Content for California and Florida

California and Florida are two of the most competitive legal markets in the country. Generic pages rarely stand out there. Content that reflects local courts, regions and client needs does.

Go regional

A firm in San Diego and a firm in Sacramento serve very different communities. In Florida, coastal cities, retirement communities and tourist areas each bring different case types.

Mind the rules

Both states have detailed attorney advertising rules. Check disclaimers, testimonials and results before publishing.
